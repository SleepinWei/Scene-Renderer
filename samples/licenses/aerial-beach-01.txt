The repository contains processed 1K diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness and
ambient occlusion textures. Each image is repacked into a vertical mip atlas;
diffuse levels average linear light, normal levels are normalized, and AO /
roughness / metallic-zero are packed into RGB. Original file URLs and checksums
are recorded in samples/beach-material.json.
