Blender benchmark scenes
=======================
Official source and license listing (checked 2026-10-05):
https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/

Classroom: Christophe Seux, CC0.
https://download.blender.org/demo/test/classroom.zip

Barcelona Pavilion: eMirage / Hamza Cheggour, CC-BY.
https://download.blender.org/demo/test/pabellon_barcelona_v1.scene_.zip
The official listing does not specify a CC-BY version; no version is assumed.
Rendered examples credit the creator and identify the conversion changes.

Changes by SceneRenderer: evaluated geometry/instances exported to a frozen
package; selected shader color graphs baked into UV atlases; shader closures,
lights and display transform adapted to the current renderer. Full Cycles shader,
compositor and physical/material equivalence are not claimed. Raw source archives
and large derived geometry/texture packages are downloaded locally outside Git.
