SceneRenderer conversion: original FBX geometry and node transforms retained;
source DDS textures decoded to PNG and optionally reduced in resolution;
DirectX BC5 normals reconstructed and converted to the OpenGL convention;
source packed green roughness and blue metallic channels mapped to native PBR;
the unused zero red occlusion placeholder ignored, matching Falcor's importer.
Native scene lighting differs from the original renderer. Large source assets
and converted files remain local, outside Git.
